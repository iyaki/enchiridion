Deterministic geometric blobatars from any string. No dependencies, about 4.4 KB.

your configBlobatar.tsx

```plain text
import { Blobatar } from "@blobatar/react";
import "blobatar/motion.css";
<Blobatar
  name="alain00"
  animate="hover"
/>
```

Every prop is optional except the name. Drop `animate` and the blobatar renders as a single `<img>`.

[Tune every trait in the editor](https://blobatar.dev/editor)

## Leave your blobataron the wall

Every blobatar here is somebody's name, and nobody chose their colour. Click an empty cell to leave yours.

## Everyone gets a face

One prop per person, and the string does the rest. The same handle always produces the same blobatar, so whoever you learn in a thread is who you recognise in the sidebar.

~4.4 KB
