## Features

What sets us apart

Flipt is designed to be simple, secure and performant and runs alongside your existing services.

Use simple on/off flags to toggle functionality in your applications or rollout features over time without requiring additional code deployments.

Flipt can be deployed within your existing infrastructure or VPC. It runs great on Kubernetes, AWS, GCP, Azure and more.

HTTPS Support. No PII leaves your environment and you don't have to open your systems to the outside world.

Optimized for performance. Since Flipt is co-located with your existing services, there's no excessive latency to slow down your applications.
