# Acapella Extractor

By using this extractor you can easily isolate vocals from any song (wav or mp3) with mixed instrumentals and vocals. This service is **free up to 2 songs per day**. This service uses artificial intelligence and is based on the open source library [spleeter](https://github.com/deezer/spleeter).

Please note that we can only make acapellas from songs up to a length of 10 minutes and 80MB - the limitation is necessary, so as not to saturate our server. You can check out examples of isolated vocals in the [examples section](https://www.acapella-extractor.com/en/examples/).

This service does not require any software or registration. By using it you are accepting the [terms of use](https://www.acapella-extractor.com/en/terms-of-use).

To isolate the vocals, upload a song from your computer. You will be redirected to the download page. As soon as the processing is finished you can download the file by clicking on the download button.

Enjoy! We hope you like our service.
