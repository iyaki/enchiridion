# Sustainability and Paperless Signatures

At SignFree, we believe in the power of technology to make the world a better place.

That's why we've created a platform that allows you to sign and manage documents online, without ever having to print a single piece of paper. By going paperless with SignFree, you're not only making your life easier, but you're also making a positive impact on the environment.

Here below there are just a few of the ways that a paperless world can help create a more sustainable future.

## Reduced deforestation.

Every year, millions of trees are cut down to produce paper. By reducing the demand for paper, we can help protect our forests and the animals that call them home.

<!-- image hosted by Notion: its URL expires and is not preserved (ADR-05) -->

## Lower carbon emissions.

The paper industry is responsible for a significant amount of greenhouse gas emissions. By going paperless, we can help reduce our carbon footprint and mitigate the impacts of climate change.

## Less waste.

Every year, billions of pounds of paper end up in landfills, where they can take decades to decompose. By using electronic documents and signatures, we can help reduce the amount of waste we produce and conserve natural resources.

## Our Story

In November 2021, On a long business trip...

Our Founder Nicola Di Giorgio found himself in a small town with no printers, a poor internet connection and an urgent need to sign various documents.

After running through the different online signature providers available...

He realized most of them made you go through so much hassle just to get a simple document signed.

Some required you to first create an account, while others limited you to a certain number of signatures on their “free” plan.

Nicola, a passionate entrepreneur who believes that technology and the internet should provide an easy way for us to grow successful businesses, decided to create SIGNFREE.

SIGNFREE is the first e-signature provider giving entrepreneurs, creatives & freelancers a fast, free & secure way to sign unlimited documents.
