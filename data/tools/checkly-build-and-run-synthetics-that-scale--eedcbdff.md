Monitoring as Code workflow for developers: programmable, fast, reliable.

## Synthetic monitoring

### Monitor crucial flows fast

Monitor and validate your crucial site transactions. Automatically collect error traces, screenshots and performance metrics with every browser check you run.

Coding browser click-flows used to be hard. Not anymore. Use modern open source frameworks like Playwright to automate your flows. Run your checks in 20 locations worldwide.

[Learn more](https://www.checklyhq.com/product/synthetic-monitoring/)

## api monitoring

### Monitor all your APIs easily

Make sure your APIs always respond quickly and with the correct payload. Our intuitive editor lets you configure powerful HTTP requests.

Kickstart your checks with our Swagger and cURL importer. Add Node.js-based setup/teardown scripts and fine-grained assertions for in-depth API monitoring.

[Learn more](https://www.checklyhq.com/product/api-monitoring/)

## Private locations

### Monitor your private applications and APIs

Get full visibility of private applications across your infrastructure, on-prem and in your private or hybrid cloud.

Run our checks wherever containers run: physical or virtual machines with a container engine such as Docker, or on your Kubernetes cluster or other orchestration platforms. Up and running in two minutes.

[Learn more](https://www.checklyhq.com/docs/private-locations/private-locations-getting-started/)

## Alerting

### Alerting when and how you want it

Keep your team up to date with a generous helping of SMS messages and unlimited email, Pagerduty, Opsgenie, Slack and webhook notifications. Control exactly how, when and how often you get alerted. "Double checking" is enabled by default to never get false positives.

[Learn more](https://www.checklyhq.com/docs/alerting)
