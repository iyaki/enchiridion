| Negative | Setting clear rules for first-party designers of Lego sets that set the tone for builders: for example, sets can’t require bricks to be assembled in ways that can’t be disassembled later | Using trade protections to keep low-quality third-party parts from entering the market, keeping the guarantee of quality for lego bricks high. |
