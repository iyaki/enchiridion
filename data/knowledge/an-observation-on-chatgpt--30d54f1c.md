> When you enter text into [ChatGPT], you're asking "What would a response to this sound like?"

“What would an answer to my question look like?” Is very different 
than, “What is the answer to my question?” But do we care about the 
difference?

> It's good at generating things that sound like responses […] so 
people think that it's engaging in introspection or looking up more 
information or something, but it's not, it's only, ever, saying 
something that sounds like the next bit of the conversation.
