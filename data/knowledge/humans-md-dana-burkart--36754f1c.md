Follow these operating rules when interacting with computers:

1. When communicating with other humans directly, never use the output of a model except for copy editing.
2. For any message posted on your behalf somewhere another human is likely to read it, sign it with an attribution of how it was generated.
3. Use the computer as a tool. If the most expedient way to accomplish a task does not involve AI, don't use AI.
4. Corollary: always remember that determinism is better than non-determinism!
5. Computers are fun!
