Gitter is a chat and networking platform that helps to manage, grow and connect communities through messaging, content and discovery.

## Built on Matrix

[Matrix.org](https://matrix.org/) is an open network for secure, decentralized communication. There is a variety of clients are available. [Learn more](https://matrix.org/clients/)

## Simple to start

Sign-in with GitHub/GitLab/Twitter and start chatting, with End-to-End Encrypted messaging.

## Markdown and LaTeX

Format your messages just like with your other favorite developer tools.

# Get Gitter

The Gitter webapp is available in all modern browsers, and there are a variety of Matrix clients available. We recommend Element for desktop, Android and iOS native apps.

After downloading Element, be sure to specify `gitter.im` as the homeserver.
