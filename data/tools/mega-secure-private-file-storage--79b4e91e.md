## Security that’s always on

We protect your data with zero-knowledge encryption, the highest level of online security and privacy. This means that only the data owner and users they authorise have the keys needed to access the data stored or shared on MEGA.

For anyone else, including MEGA, the data appears as encrypted and is impossible to read or understand.
