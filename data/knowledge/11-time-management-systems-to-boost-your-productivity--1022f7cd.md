| **System** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| [The 1-3-5 Rule](https://hubstaff.com/blog/time-management-tools/#135-rule) | Divides tasks based on their size. |
| [52 / 17](https://hubstaff.com/blog/time-management-tools/#52-17) | Work for 52 minutes and rest for 17. |
| [7 Minute Life](https://hubstaff.com/blog/time-management-tools/#7-minute-life) | A time management method that uses 1% of your day for planning. |
| [Autofocus](https://hubstaff.com/blog/time-management-tools/#autofocus) | Do the tasks you’re most motivated to do first. |
| [Bullet Journal](https://hubstaff.com/blog/time-management-tools/#bullet-journal) | A way of organizing by making use of bullet points and notations. |
| [Eisenhower Matrix](https://hubstaff.com/blog/time-management-tools/#eisenhower-matrix) | Sorts tasks by urgency. It’s great for prioritizing! |
| [Getting Things Done](https://hubstaff.com/blog/time-management-tools/#gtd) | David Allen’s famous and comprehensive time management method. |
| [Kanban](https://hubstaff.com/blog/time-management-tools/#kanban) | Group tasks into a visual representation to keep them organized. |
| [The Pomodoro Technique](https://hubstaff.com/blog/time-management-tools/#pomodoro) | 25 minutes of work followed by a 5-minute break to maintain focus. |
| [Time Blocking](https://hubstaff.com/blog/time-management-tools/#time-blocking) | Allocates hours of the day for specific tasks to plan better. |
| [Time Tracking](https://hubstaff.com/blog/time-management-tools/#time-tracking) | Increase productivity by tracking hours and monitoring tasks. |
