## EASILY ADOPT

Cycles and Modules, a better way to use any frameworks

Simplify your transition to Waterfall, Agile, and other workflows to keep up team's momentum with Cycles and Modules.

Conquer with Cycles. Cycles in Plane is a custom time period for completing backlog items, enabling efficient planning and prioritization.Divide into Modules. With Modules, create focused projects that enable grouping and organization of issues within a specific time frame.Better Insights. Frameworks on Plane provide deeper insights into Cycles and Modules, including burn-down charts, estimates, scope, and more.
