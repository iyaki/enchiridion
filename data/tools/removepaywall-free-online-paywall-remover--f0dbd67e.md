### What sites does this work for?

We are constantly working on adding new sites and improving the paywall remover. The website works by finding an archived version of a page, so it is hard to tell. Currently, I have tested the website on a little over a hundred different news websites. However, most websites do archive their articles. This means that ideally, this should work for most sites. Of course, if there is an issue please report it so we can fix it.
