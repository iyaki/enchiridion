## Early Access Game

Get instant access and start playing; get involved with this game as it develops.

Note: Games in Early Access are not complete and may or may not change further. If you are not excited to play this game in its current state, then you should wait to see if the game progresses further in development. Learn more

What the developers have to say:

### Why Early Access?

“Player feedback is crucial for puzzle games. Each level starts with an idea I want the player to realize on their own, but it takes a lot of trial and error to figure out how to get the difficulty of each level right.”

### Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?

“The game will exit early access in 2026”

### How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?

“The release version will have a more powerful assembler, a more realistic campaign and simulate MUCH faster”

### What is the current state of the Early Access version?

“The game is definitely playable, with 70+ levels and 50+ hours of play (except of course if you are already very familiar with the subjects).”

### Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?

“As the game matures I expect to increase the price.”

### How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process?

“I am actively posting on our discord. You can find the latest information about the release version of the game there”
