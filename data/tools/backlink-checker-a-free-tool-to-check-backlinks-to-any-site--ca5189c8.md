## What Are Backlinks?

A backlink in SEO is a link from one website to another. They’re also called inbound links, one-way links, or incoming links. If someone links their web page to your website, that means you have a backlink from them. And if you link other pages to your website, then they have a backlink from you.

Major search engines like Google consider backlinks as “votes” for a specific page. The higher the number of backlinks you have, the higher you would rank in the search engines organically. In a way, backlinks tell search engines: “this page/website is relevant, helpful, and credible.”

There are several types of backlinks, a few of them are:

- Guest blogging links
- Press release links
- Editorial links
- Comment links
- Free tool links

How do you actually improve your backlink quality? And how can you ensure you are only adding valuable backlinks to your site?

### Keywords and Anchor Texts

Anchor texts refer to the clickable words used to create website backlinks. Typically, anchor texts provide link context. There are several types of anchor text links, including:

- Exact Match
- The anchor text uses the exact words, keywords, or phrases for which you want to rank.
- Phrase Match
- The anchor text contains the keyword for which you want to rank
- Branded
- The anchor text is the brand’s name
- Naked
- The anchor text is the raw URL

In general, it’s always a best practice to add links by keyword. Hence, keyword research may also play a part in doing this.

### Do-Follow Links VS No-Follow Links

You may have encountered the words dofollow and nofollow before. What are they exactly?

When a link is a dofollow, it passes the authority of website A to website B. This concept is called “link juice.” Adding do-follow links to your website will help improve its domain rating. New links added to your site are dofollow by default.

No-follow links, on the other hand, do not pass the authority to the website it is linking to. That said, they do not help in ranking in terms of SEO. You can use no-follow links if you do not want to endorse the linked site or when you do not want to pass its authority.

To turn a link into a nofollow, simply add the tag rel=”no follow” to the code when linking to a webpage.

### How Do I Find my Competitors' Backlinks?

There are a few different ways you can find your competitors' backlinks:

1. Use a backlink analysis tool: There are many tools available that can help you see who is linking to your competitors' websites. Some popular options include Ahrefs, SEMrush, and Moz's Link Explorer. These tools allow you to enter your competitor's website URL and see a list of the websites that are linking to it.
2. Check your competitor's website: Some websites display their backlinks on their website, either in the footer or on a dedicated "Partners" or "Links" page. You can check your competitor's website for these links to see who is linking to them.
3. Use Google's search operator "link:": You can use Google's "link:" search operator to see which websites are linking to your competitor's website. For example, if your competitor's website is example.com, you can search for "link:example.com" in Google to see a list of websites that are linking to it.
4. Check social media: Your competitors may also be sharing their backlinks on social media platforms like Twitter or LinkedIn. You can check their social media accounts to see if they are sharing links to other websites.

It's important to note that not all backlink analysis tools and techniques will provide a complete list of a website's backlinks. Some backlinks may be hidden or not easily accessible. However, using a combination of these methods can help you get a good idea of your competitors' backlink profile.

### Why Are Backlinks Important in SEO?

Backlinks, also known as inbound links or incoming links, are links from other websites that point to your website. They are important for search engine optimization (SEO) because they signal to search engines that your website is valuable, relevant, and trustworthy.

When a website links to another website, it is effectively giving a vote of confidence to that website and telling search engines that it is a good resource for users. As a result, websites with a large number of high-quality backlinks tend to rank higher in search engine results pages (SERPs).

Here are a few reasons why backlinks are important for SEO:

1. They help improve your website's visibility in search engines: When a website links to your site, it can help increase your site's visibility in search engines like Google. This is because search engines view backlinks as a sign of the quality and relevance of your website.
2. They help drive traffic to your website: Backlinks can also help drive traffic to your website, as users may click on the links to visit your site. This can be especially helpful if the linking website has a high number of visitors.
3. They can improve your website's domain authority: Domain authority is a metric used by search engines to determine how trustworthy and authoritative a website is. Websites with a high domain authority tend to rank higher in search results. Backlinks from high-authority websites can help increase your own website's domain authority.

It's important to note that not all backlinks are created equal. Search engines place more value on backlinks from high-quality, authoritative websites than from low-quality or spammy websites. Therefore, it's important to focus on building backlinks from reputable sources.

### Are Backlink Checker Tools Accurate?

Backlink checker tools can be a useful resource for tracking and analyzing the backlinks pointing to your website, but it's important to note that they may not be completely accurate. This is because not all backlinks are easily accessible or visible to these tools.

There are a few reasons why backlink checker tools may not be completely accurate:

1. Some backlinks may be hidden or not easily accessible: Some websites may hide their backlinks using techniques like JavaScript or nofollow tags, which can make them difficult for backlink checker tools to detect.
2. Not all backlink checker tools have access to the same data: Different backlink checker tools may have access to different data sources, which can lead to discrepancies in the results they provide.
3. Backlink data can be slow to update: Backlink data can take time to propagate across the web, which means that some backlinks may not be detected by backlink checker tools until they have had time to update their databases.

Despite these limitations, backlink checker tools can still be a useful resource for tracking and analyzing your backlinks. It's important to use multiple tools and techniques to get a comprehensive view of your backlink profile, and to keep in mind that no tool will be 100% accurate.

### Can Backlinks be Hidden from a Backlink Checker Tool?

Yes, it is possible for backlinks to be hidden from a backlink checker. There are a few ways that websites can hide their backlinks:

1. Using JavaScript: Some websites may use JavaScript to display their backlinks, which can make them difficult for backlink checker tools to detect. This is because most backlink checker tools are not able to execute JavaScript, so they are unable to see the links that are being displayed on the page.
2. Using nofollow tags: Websites can also use the "nofollow" attribute in their HTML code to instruct search engines not to follow a particular link. This can make the link invisible to backlink checker tools, as they rely on search engines to discover and crawl links.
3. Hiding links in images: Websites can also hide their backlinks by using images as links, rather than text. This can make the links difficult for backlink checker tools to detect, as they are not able to "read" images in the same way that they can read text.

It's important to note that while these techniques can make it more difficult for backlink checker tools to detect certain links, they may still be discoverable through other means. For example, links that are hidden using JavaScript may still be visible if you view the page source or use a tool that is able to execute JavaScript.
