**Who uses Blot?**

Some of the people who use Blot to create their website

Blot converts files you put inside a folder into posts on your website. You can connect your folder to Blot with:

- [Dropbox: Use a folder in your Dropbox](https://blot.im/how/sync/dropbox)
- [Git: Use a git repository](https://blot.im/how/sync/git)
- [Google Drive: Use a folder in your Drive](https://blot.im/how/sync/google-drive)

All of these files become posts automatically when you place them in your blog’s folder:

- [Text and Markdown](https://blot.im/how/posts/text-and-markdown)[.txt .md](https://blot.im/how/posts/text-and-markdown)
- [Images](https://blot.im/how/posts/images)[.png .jpeg .gif](https://blot.im/how/posts/images)
- [HTML](https://blot.im/how/posts/html)[.html](https://blot.im/how/posts/html)

[**About**](https://blot.im/about)

Blot is a blogging platform with no interface. It turns a folder into a website. Files inside your blog’s folder become blog posts automatically. Blot exists so that you can blog with your favorite tools. Use your favorite text-editor to write and Blot does the rest.
