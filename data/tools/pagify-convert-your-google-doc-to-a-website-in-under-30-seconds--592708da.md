### Step 1

In your Google Doc, click **File** → **Publish to the web**.

![image](https://www.gopagify.com/img/steps/step1.png)

Click File, then Publish to the web.

### Step 2

In the popup, click **Publish**, then **OK**.

![image](https://www.gopagify.com/img/steps/step2_mod.png)

The Publish to web popup in Google Docs.

### Step 3

Copy the link you see (or copy the doc's URL) and paste it below.

![image](https://www.gopagify.com/img/steps/step3_mod.png)

Copy the link in the popup.

## Tips & Tricks

- For better formatting on mobile, set the margins to 0.25” all around in **File** → **Page Setup**.
- You can also change the background color in **File** → **Page Setup**.
- The Google Docs [templates](https://docs.google.com/templates) are great starting points for your website!
- You can download the Google Docs app to make changes on the go.
