# A personal user manual: How to work with me

**Manual of Me** is a personal user manual - a document which helps others understand how they can work best with you.

It's a living document which not explains how you work, how to work brilliantly with you, what value you bring, your preferences, needs and motivations;

It's a platform to help you explore your own working needs and preferences and be more able to communicate them with others.

It's a framework to uncover insights, prompt conversations, and build stronger and more supportive relationships at work.

It's a way we can all consider, communicate and celebrate our differences, and work better together.
