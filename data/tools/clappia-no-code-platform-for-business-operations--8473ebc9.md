## Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the product.

What is Clappia and how does it work?

Clappia is a no-code platform that enables users to build custom business applications (mobile and web) without requiring any programming knowledge. It works by providing an intuitive drag-and-drop interface that allows users to design forms, workflows, and dashboards.

What are the benefits of using Clappia for building no-code apps?

The benefits of using Clappia for building no-code apps include increased efficiency, faster development times, and the ability to customize applications to suit specific business needs. Additionally, Clappia's cloud-based platform eliminates the need for costly IT infrastructure and maintenance.

Is Clappia suitable for non-technical users?

Yes, Clappia is designed to be user-friendly and accessible to non-technical users. The platform's drag-and-drop interface and pre-built templates make it easy for anyone to create custom business applications without requiring coding knowledge.

How does Clappia ensure the security and privacy of our data?

Clappia takes the security and privacy of user data seriously and employs a variety of measures to protect sensitive information. These include encryption of data, regular security audits and assessments, disaster recovery measures and granular access controls to ensure that only authorized users can access data. Additionally, Clappia is hosted on AWS (Amazon Web Services), which offers a robust and secure infrastructure with high standards for data protection.

Can I integrate Clappia with other tools and services?

Clappia offers seamless integration with a wide range of tools and services, including popular platforms such as Zapier, Google Sheets, Google Drive, Slack, MS Teams, Power BI, Whatsapp, SMS, MySQL, Azure SQL, and many others. Additionally, Clappia provides REST APIs, which enable users to easily integrate their applications with any other systems they may be using. This level of integration allows users to automate workflows and data transfer between different applications and platforms, streamlining business processes and increasing productivity.

How is Clappia different from Low Code platforms?

Clappia and low-code platforms have some similarities, but differ in key ways. Low-code platforms require some coding and technical knowledge, while Clappia is completely no-code. Low-code platforms offer advanced features but can be costly and require specialized knowledge. Clappia is more user-friendly, offering a streamlined interface and flexibility that makes it accessible to businesses of all sizes and technical abilities.
